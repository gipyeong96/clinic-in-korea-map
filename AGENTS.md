# Care guide and blog editorial preferences

- For articles about consulting clients, consult the hospital's official English website before drafting. Use relevant condition, examination, treatment, equipment and branch pages to make the article specific and useful.
- Distinguish services described by the hospital from independently sourced general medical information. Verify the latter with authoritative clinical sources.
- Explain practical selection criteria, relevant questions for a consultation and what to confirm at the branch. Avoid generic copy, unsupported superiority claims, guaranteed outcomes or invented branch-specific capabilities.
- Link directly to relevant official condition/service pages and record the source and check date. Disclose the consulting relationship.
- Do not infer English-speaking clinicians or interpretation availability from international-patient registration.
- Research is read-only unless the user also asks to change hospital records or publish an article.
